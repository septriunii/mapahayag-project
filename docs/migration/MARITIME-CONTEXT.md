# Sea and ocean labels — local review

The map retains the existing sea/ocean label styling, geographic anchors, scale
thresholds and collision avoidance. EEZ, PAR and archipelagic baseline outlines,
the boundary legend, boundary fetching and unused boundary assets were removed
at the user's request. Administrative boundaries and drilldown are unchanged.

The national viewing extent remains 112.2–131.2 E, 2.3–25.3 N to leave space for
water labels. Labels follow pan/zoom and avoid land and administrative text.
Crowded labels are suppressed; smaller local seas appear as the map is zoomed.
Municipal views omit water labels. No new network request is needed for labels.

## Official naming references

- https://www.namria.gov.ph/jdownloads/Annual_Report/2019_NAMRIA_Annual_Report.pdf — West Philippine Sea, Philippine Sea, Sulu Sea and Celebes Sea.
- https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/learning-tools/philippine-area-of-responsibility — Pacific Ocean context.
- https://www.namria.gov.ph/jdownloads/Navigational_Warning/NAVPHIL_031_2022.pdf — Bohol Sea, Visayan Sea and Sibuyan Sea.
- https://www.namria.gov.ph/jdownloads/Navigational_Warning/NAVPHIL_030_2022.pdf — Camotes Sea.

Anchors are editorial label positions in the named bodies of water, not official
centroids or sea boundaries. No maritime jurisdiction is inferred from names.
